What we check in a business cover review

No two businesses carry the same risks. These are the areas most of our reviews end up working through.

Cover for your key people

When the person who wins the work, holds the client relationships or has the hard-to-replace skill is suddenly off, this pays to bridge the gap while you regroup.

Set by the value that person brings in

Shareholder and partner buyouts

Funds a fair purchase of an owner’s stake if they die or fall seriously ill, so the owners left behind are not paying for it out of their own pockets.

Built to match your ownership agreement

Keeping business debt covered

The bank still expects its repayments when a key person is out of action. This cover pays down or keeps up loans and overdrafts if that happens.

Sized to what the business has borrowed

Your own pay as the owner

Plenty of owners only get paid when they can show up and do the work. It is worth knowing what would happen to your income if you could not.

Checked together with the business cover
